Source mockups
==============

Full-resolution PNG originals for the three case-study mockups.

These are NOT served: they are the master files the optimised, cropped JPGs in
public/projects were generated from (2000px wide, ~200KB each instead of 15MB
total). Keep them here, outside public/, so a deploy does not ship 15MB of
imagery that nothing references.

To regenerate the web versions, crop to the device composition and export at
2000px wide as JPG (quality 82), then drop them into public/projects/.
